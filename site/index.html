        The rules it follows
        
      
        Rule IDs appear on every event in the inbox, so you can trace any action back to one of these.
        
    
        
    
        
      
        
        
        Inbox
        
        
        R1 Never answer itself
        Comments by the Page and message echoes are ignored, so the agent cannot reply to its own replies.
        R2 Threats go to a person at once
        When the rules see a threat, the campaign manager is alerted and nothing is sent. The comment is hidden only when both checks agree, and the payload is kept. When only the model sees one, it goes to the top of your queue.
        R3 Press goes to comms
        Reporters get a person. A DM gets a short holding reply inside the 24-hour window when both checks agree.
        R4 Voting misinformation is corrected with the official source
        Comms is alerted, and when both checks agree the reply points to vote.gov. The comment is never hidden automatically.
        R5 Voting logistics only from a fixed template
        Polling places, hours, ID and registration: the reply links to vote.gov. The model never writes dates, hours or places.
        R6 Spam, scams and prompt injection
        Hidden when both checks agree. Instructions inside a comment are treated as text, never followed.
        R7 Abuse on an incumbent's page goes to a person
        Candidate and committee pages can auto-hide insults. On an officeholder's page, hiding critics can count as state action (Lindke v. Freed, 2024), so a person decides.
        R8 Criticism is never hidden
        Disagreement and anything unclear goes to a person. No automatic reply.
        R9 Policy answers wait for approval
        Drafts use only the client's approved positions and always wait for a person to approve them.
        R10 Two checks must agree
        Nothing is sent or hidden without a person unless the rules and the model give the same intent.
        R11 Messenger windows
        Automated DMs only within 24 hours of the person's last message (up to 7 days after a click-to-Messenger ad). HUMAN_AGENT tag only on a person's reply within 7 days.
        R12 One private reply per comment, within 7 days
        Meta allows a single private reply to a comment, sent within 7 days of it.
        R13 Right client, right links
        Every link must be on that client's allowlist, and text may not name another client. The Page token must match the Page that received the event.
        R14 Automated replies say so
        The first automated DM in a thread says it is automated; automated public replies are tagged. California's bot law (SB 1001) covers bots used to influence a vote.
        R15 Rate limit
        At most 20 automated public replies per Page per hour; the rest wait for a person.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Posts and ads
        
        
        C1 Right client
        Text may not name another client of the consultant, and the Page token must be this client's.
        C2 Allowed links only
        Every link must be on the client's allowlist.
        C3 Voting details point to the official source
        A post that gives a voting date, hours or method must link to vote.gov or the election office.
        C4 Schedule window
        Page posts can be scheduled 10 minutes to 30 days ahead (Pages API guide).
        C5 Political category and disclaimer
        Ads must use special_ad_categories ISSUES_ELECTIONS_POLITICS, a country, and the client's "Paid for by" disclaimer.
        C6 AI media disclosure
        Realistic AI-made or altered people, voices or events need authorization_category POLITICAL_WITH_DIGITALLY_CREATED_MEDIA.
        C7 No Meta generative AI ad features
        Meta's own generative AI ad creative tools are not available for these ads, so the build never requests them.
        C8 No EU delivery
        Meta stopped ads about social issues, elections or politics in the EU on Oct 6, 2025.
        C9 Restriction period
        New US political ads cannot launch during Meta's final-week restriction period unless they delivered an impression before it began.
        
        
        Where the rules come from
        
        
        1. Messenger Platform policy: 24-hour window, up to 7 days after a click-to-Messenger ad, Human Agent, private replies, automation disclosure
        2. Private Replies: one message per comment, within 7 days
        3. Webhooks: X-Hub-Signature-256 on every event
        4. Meta signs an escaped-unicode form of the payload (Hookdeck guide)
        5. Pages API: schedule posts 10 minutes to 30 days ahead
        6. Comment node: is_hidden, MODERATE task, pages_manage_engagement
        7. Special Ad Categories: ISSUES_ELECTIONS_POLITICS, country, authorization_category
        8. Meta and the 2026 midterms: authorization, "Paid for by", Ad Library for 7 years, restriction period from Oct 27, 2026
        9. Ads about social issues, elections or politics: AI disclosure, automated detection from June 1, 2026, no EU delivery
        10. Meta ends political ads in the EU (Oct 6, 2025)
        11. Meta's own generative AI ad tools unavailable for political ads (summary of the Business Help Center, checked Sep 2026)
        12. Ad Library API (ads_archive)
        13. Lindke v. Freed (2024): when an official deleting comments is state action
        14. California SB 1001: bots used to influence a vote
        15. Learning phase: about 50 optimization events in 7 days; large budget edits reset it
        16. Meta's ads MCP server (2026)
        
      
        
    
        
  
        

        


        
  
        
    
        What was measured, and what was not
        
  
        
  
        
    
        
      
        Measured
        
      
          
        
        • Held-out set (79 messages, written before the rules): rules 79/79, model 50/79. When both agreed (50 times) they were right 50 times. Through the full pipeline: 25 answered, 8 hidden, 18/18 threats, press and misinformation reached a person, 0 false alerts, 14 extra items flagged by the model alone, 0 wrong automatic actions, 0 requests refused by the mock Graph.
          • 
        
        • Stress set (32 messages with typos, sarcasm and leetspeak, written after the rules were frozen): rules 4/32, model 19/32, 0 replies sent, 7/7 threats, press and misinformation reached a person (all through the model's flag, since the rules missed them), 31 handed to a person, 1 wrong action.
          • 
        
        • Fuzz with random AI opinions and random timing: 8,440 trials on the normal sets gave 0 forbidden actions out of 384 taken; 1,280 trials on the stress set led to 15 automatic actions and 15 were wrong, each where the rules had misread the message and the random opinion happened to agree. A no-checks agent: 8,866.
          • 
        
        • Live HTTP: 1,368 requests at 16 at a time, 902 per second, acknowledgement p50 16.6 ms, p95 27.9 ms, p99 46.5 ms. Handshake correct, wrong verify token refused (403).
          • 
        
        • Unit tests on Meta's windows, private replies, tokens, signatures, checks and pacing: 28 of 28 pass; live HTTP test: 1 of 1.
          • 
      
        
    
        
    
        
      
        Not measured
        
      
          
        
        • No real Facebook Page, Messenger thread or ad account was used. The Graph API is a mock that enforces the documented rules; its error codes are its own.
          • 
        
        • No language model ran. The second opinion here is a small Naive Bayes model trained on 132 example messages; the Claude and OpenAI adapters and the scoring script are included but were not run without a key.
          • 
        
        • All three message sets were written by the same author as the rules, so real comments will score lower. The stress result shows how it fails: to a person, not out loud.
          • 
        
        • The ad market is simulated with assumed costs. Real lift depends on the campaign.
          • 
        
        • Meta App Review, Business Verification and political ad authorization take time and are not shown.
